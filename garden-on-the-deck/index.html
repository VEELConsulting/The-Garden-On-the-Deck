About the garden

This garden was cultivated by Mama Udofia.

The garden on the deck began with a few pots and a sunny corner. It has grown into a small, devoted harvest of peppers, some that burn bright and some as gentle as summer.

Everything here is raised in containers, watered by hand and picked when it is ready. Nothing is hurried, and nothing is wasted.
